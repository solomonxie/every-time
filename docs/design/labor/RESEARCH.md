# Labor — research

The evidence behind every rule in `LaborAnalysis`. ⚠ = not confirmed from the source itself (ACOG, NICE and Mayo pages blocked fetching; secondary sources used).

## Measuring
- Interval: start → next start. Length: start → end (Cleveland Clinic, Kaiser).
- Time for about 1 h to see a pattern (Cleveland Clinic).
- Intensity has no clinical scale. The proxy is "can't walk or talk through it". The app's mild/moderate/strong is a self-report.
- Length 20–70 s, depending on stage (Cleveland Clinic).

## When to call / go
| Rule | Who |
|---|---|
| 5-1-1: ≤5 min apart, ~1 min long, for 1 h | Sutter, Cleveland Clinic, ACNM handout; ACOG ⚠ (ACOG patient text: time for an hour, then call) |
| 4-1-1 | Boston Medical Center (first births); Scripps (later births) |
| 3-1-1 | Scripps; no national body ⚠ |
| Later births: every 5–7 min, 45–60 s | Kaiser; BMC 5–6 min |
| Regular and ≤5 min apart → call | NHS |
| >30 min from hospital → go at 10 min apart | Quebec INSPQ |
| Always: the care team's own instruction wins | all |

No single rule is endorsed by ACOG or NICE → configurable, 5-1-1 default.

## Phases (defined by cervix, not timing)
- Active labor starts at 6 cm (ACOG/SMFM 2014), 5 cm (WHO 2018) or 4 cm (NICE NG235).
- Early/latent: contractions every 5–30+ min, 20–45 s, for 6–12 h, often days (NHS GGC). Prolonged if >20 h for a first birth, >14 h for later births.
- Active: every 3–5 min, 40–70 s, for 4–8 h. WHO: usually ≤12 h for a first birth, ≤10 h for later births.
- "Transition" isn't a formal phase anywhere ⚠. Timing at its peak: every 2–3 min, 60–90 s.
- Second stage: every 2–5 min, 60–90 s. ≥3 h of pushing allowed for a first birth, ≥2 h for later births (ACOG/SMFM).
- Prodromal labor: contractions as often as every 5 min, up to 1 min long, can last days, no dilation. Only a cervical exam tells it apart (Cleveland Clinic).
- Most first-time mothers after 2 h of regular contractions weren't yet in active labor (J Fam Pract 2009). Timing can't confirm a stage.

## Braxton Hicks vs labor
| Braxton Hicks | Labor |
|---|---|
| irregular | regular |
| not closer, not stronger | closer, longer, stronger |
| ease with rest, water, position change | keep going whatever you do |
| often weak | hard to talk through |

- Evidence: a shortening interval predicts labor (Ragusa 2005, OR 1.42). "Regular" alone wasn't significant.
- No published numeric threshold (e.g. CV of intervals) exists. The app's regularity cut-offs are its own, labelled as such.

## Red flags — call now, whatever the timing
- Waters broke, above all green/brown or smelly fluid (NHS). Mayo: go in even if unsure.
- Bleeding heavier than a pink show / like a period (NHS, Mayo).
- Baby moving less: call now, even at night. No count needed (NHS, CDC).
- Before 37 weeks: ≥4 contractions in 1 h that don't stop with rest (Cleveland Clinic). Sutter says 5, NC 6. Mayo: any labor sign.
- A contraction >2 min, or ≥6 in 10 min (NHS).
- Constant severe pain; fever ≥38 °C; bad headache, vision changes, swelling of the face or hands, chest pain, trouble breathing, fainting (CDC Hear Her).
- Urge to push → emergency (NHS).

## Plan setup (travel, transport, conditions)
| Setup answer | App rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| >30 min away (incl. ride wait) | go at 10 min apart | INSPQ |
| Later baby | ≤5 min apart, no full hour (app: 20 min = 4 gaps) | INSPQ |
| Previous labor <3 h | 10 min apart | precipitous = <3 h (Radiopaedia); "leave earlier" — Active Birth Centre ⚠ |
| GBS positive | call at first contractions or waters; antibiotics ASAP, ≥4 h optimal | RCOG; ACOG CO 797 |
| Previous C-section | call as soon as labor starts or waters break | RCOG VBAC leaflet |
| Planned C-section | ~1 in 10 labor first → call straight away | Oxford UH |
| Breech | cord prolapse risk → call early | NHS Scotland |
| Twins, high BP, other | call early ⚠ (conservative; defer to care team) | Oxford UH (twins) |
| Before 37 weeks | any sign → call | March of Dimes |
| Rideshare / pick-up | +15 min wait (app's own estimate); book taxis "in good time" | nidirect |
| Urge to push, cord visible, heavy bleeding | don't drive: get low, call 911 / 120 / 999 | NIAS, U of Utah Health |
| Sent home in early labor | common, normal (~60% of one triage cohort) | Sutter, NHS, UT Southwestern |
| Waters + fever >37.5 °C | report | NHS trusts |

⚠ No official rideshare rule, no 20/45/60 min thresholds, no official "must not drive herself" statement were found.

## Other apps
- Full Term: interval, length, intensity, 1–6 h summaries, 5-1-1 or custom alert.
- Contraction Timer IO: early/active/transition label from timing, 5-1-1 alert, "not a medical device".
- Common: averages over the last 20 min vs the last hour, as a trend.
