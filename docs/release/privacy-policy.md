# Privacy Policy — Every Time

_Last updated: 2026-10-02_

Every Time does not collect, transmit, or store your personal data on any server we control. We operate no server and have no accounts.

## What the app stores
Your cities, countdowns, events, timers, work log, activity log, naps and nights you log, jet lag trips and settings are kept on your device. They stay there unless you choose to back them up or export them.

## Apple Health
- Read-only, and only after you tap "Add nights from Health" and allow it. The app never writes to Health.
- It reads sleep analysis (asleep, in bed, sleep stages) for the last 30 days to show past nights and estimate your typical sleep-cycle length.
- Health data is used only on your device to show you your sleep. It is never sent anywhere, never used for advertising or marketing, never shared with third parties, and never included in backups or iCloud Drive.

## Calendar
- Calendar, only if you allow it: events are read on the device so you can mark one as important, and lunar events you choose are added to your calendar.
- Nothing from your calendar leaves the device.

## Notifications and alarms
Countdown, event, lunar, wind-down, jet lag and nap alarms are scheduled locally on the device. No push server is involved.

## Widgets
The app shares a small snapshot (your cities, upcoming countdowns, sleep times) with its own widget. It does not leave your device.

## What leaves your device, and only if you ask
- **iCloud Drive backup** — off by default. When on, backup files are written to your own iCloud Drive (Files → iCloud Drive → Every Time), in your Apple account and your storage quota. We have no access to them.
- **Export / share** — backup or CSV files you share go wherever you choose.
- **On this day** — opening this tool requests the month and day you picked from Wikipedia (Wikimedia Foundation). No personal data is included; Wikimedia's privacy policy covers that request. Content is from Wikipedia under CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Analytics and advertising
None. No analytics SDK, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers, no tracking across apps or websites.

## Children
The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Deleting your data
Deleting the app removes its data from the device. Backups in your own iCloud Drive or exported files remain yours to delete. Health access can be revoked any time in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Every Time.

Material changes to this policy will be published here with a new date.
